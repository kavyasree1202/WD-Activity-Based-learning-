🛡️ Safety Guidelines

Your safety comes first.

  • 🧭 Follow your guide's instructions.
  • 🥾 Stay on marked trails.
  • 💧 Carry sufficient drinking water.
  • 👥 Avoid trekking alone.
  • 🌦️ Check weather forecasts before departure.
  • 🩹 Carry first-aid supplies.
  • 📱 Inform someone about your itinerary.
  • ⛰️ Turn back if conditions become unsafe.

Follow local advisories and your guide's safety decisions.
